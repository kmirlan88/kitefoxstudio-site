Kitefox Uploader

Kitefox Uploader is the tool Kitefox Studio uses to publish its own music and videos to its own YouTube channels, starting with Толкун (@tolkunmusic).

Kitefox Uploader uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and your data is also covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

What it does

For each release we prepare, it signs in to our channel with Google and:

  • uploads the video, or picks up one we uploaded in YouTube Studio;
  • sets the title, description, language, category, audience and AI disclosure;
  • adds the thumbnail, subtitles and playlist;
  • publishes the video or schedules it, then checks that YouTube saved it.

Who can use it

Only Kitefox Studio. It runs on our own computer and acts only on channels we own. It is not offered, sold or distributed to anyone else, and it does not read or change other people's channels or videos.

Your data

Read the Privacy Policy for exactly what it accesses and stores, and the Terms of Service. To stop it from accessing a Google account, remove “Kitefox Uploader” in Google Account → Third-party connections.
